01 / BASIC CAR WASH

START
WITH RIGHT.

A great finish starts with a great wash. Regular care removes road film, dirt and contaminants before they have time to dull the surfaces you love.

We use professional Autoglym, GYEON and Gtechniq products with paint-safe technique - no harsh shortcuts. A regular weekly or fortnightly wash keeps paint, trim, wheels and glass looking fresher, and makes deeper detailing easier to maintain.

  • Safe exterior wash and pre-rinse
  • Wheels, tyres and arches cleaned
  • Glass and tyre finish
  • Full cabin and mat vacuum
  • Seats, carpets and touchpoints cleaned
  • Leather protection where applicable
More than an exterior rinse.

Your wash also resets the cabin: vacuuming, interior surface cleaning, carpet attention and leather care where suitable. The result is a car that feels as good inside as it looks outside.

02 / INTERIOR + EXTERIOR DETAILING

DETAILS
MAKE THE CAR.

Detailing is the focused work that takes a car beyond clean. We tailor it to the condition of the cabin, paint and the standard you want to return to.

Interior and exterior detailing are different disciplines. One restores the place you sit; the other enhances the surfaces that meet the road and the light.

Interior detailing

Deep vacuuming, detailed cleaning of plastics and touchpoints, upholstery and carpet treatment where required, interior glass, leather care and odour-focused attention.

Exterior detailing

Safe wash and decontamination, wheels and tyres, paint cleansing, trim treatment, glass care and a protection finish selected for your vehicle.

What it is not.

Detailing is not automatically paint correction. Correction is a separate polishing process for visible paint defects, assessed only when it suits your paint and the outcome you want.

03 / CERAMIC COATING

GLOSS THAT
GOES FURTHER.

Ceramic coating is a liquid protection applied to properly prepared paint. It cures into a durable, water-repelling layer that enhances gloss and makes washing easier.

It is not invincible, but it is a brilliant protection choice for owners who want a clean, high-gloss finish that is easier to look after between professional visits.

  • Enhanced depth, gloss and clarity
  • Water beading and easier cleaning
  • Resistance to daily grime and contamination
  • Options for paint, trim and wheels
Make the coating last.

Use pH-neutral shampoo, clean microfibre towels and regular gentle washes. Avoid automatic brush washes, remove bird droppings and tree sap quickly, and book maintenance inspections when needed.

04 / PAINT CORRECTION

SEE YOUR
PAINT AGAIN.

Paint correction is a measured machine-polishing process that reduces visible swirls, haze, oxidation and light-to-moderate surface defects.

We inspect your paint before we begin, then match the correction approach to its condition and your goals. The reward is clearer reflections, richer colour and a stronger foundation for protection.

  • Reduced light swirls and haze
  • Improved clarity and paint depth
  • Refined gloss and sharper reflections
  • Excellent preparation for ceramic coating
Necessary when the finish looks tired.

A clean car can still look dull when fine defects scatter the light. Correction restores visual clarity. It cannot promise to remove every deep scratch, so we set expectations after inspecting the car.

05 / VINYL WRAPS

MAKE IT
YOUR OWN.

Vinyl wrap is a precisely fitted film that changes your vehicle's colour, texture or styling without a permanent respray. The palette is enormous: gloss, satin, matte, metallic, colour-shift and textured finishes.

Quality film forms a sacrificial cosmetic layer over sound original paint and can help shield it from light scuffs and UV exposure. It is not the same as dedicated PPF, which is made for stronger impact protection. Professional removal is designed not to damage healthy factory paint.

3M Wrap Film Avery Dennison ORAFOL Hexis
Studio-only installation.

Vinyl wraps are not mobile. Your car is parked in a clean, enclosed workspace so each panel can be prepared, applied and checked in controlled conditions. Timing varies with the coverage and complexity of the vehicle.
